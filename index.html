Custom orders

Get something made just for you.

Every commission is one-on-one. Here's how it usually goes.

  1. 1

    Tell us the idea

    A photo, a logo, fan art, letters, a name: whatever you want stitched, and what you'd like it on.
  2. 2

    Get a quote & mockup

    We'll follow up by email with pricing, timing, and a look at the design before anything is stitched.
  3. 3

    It gets stitched

    Once it's approved, your piece is digitized, embroidered, finished, and packed with care.
  4. 4

    It ships to you

    You'll get a note when it's on its way.

About the studio

A small studio, made by hand.

Chen Art Co. is a one-person embroidery studio. Every design gets drawn up, digitized, and stitched in-house on a home embroidery machine, one order at a time. What started as gifts for friends turned into custom stoles, sweatshirts, and keepsakes for anyone with an idea worth stitching down.

No two orders look the same, and that's the point. Describe it, draw it, or send a screenshot, and it can probably be embroidered.
