What we do

Every piece starts as your idea.

Send a reference, a design, or just a vibe — a family crest, a favorite character, your organization's letters, a name you want to keep close. It gets digitized and stitched onto the garment of your choice, on a home embroidery machine, one piece at a time.

Custom orders

About the studio

A small studio, made by hand.

Chen Art Co is a one-person embroidery studio — every design is drawn up, digitized, and stitched in-house, on a home embroidery machine, order by order. What started as gifts for friends turned into custom stoles, sweatshirts, and keepsakes for anyone with an idea worth stitching down.

No two orders look the same, and that's the point — if you can describe it (or draw it, or send a screenshot), it can probably be embroidered.
